About us

We’re Thorsten and Jonah, father and son, and the founders of .

The idea came up over a flat white in Vienna. Jonah studies there and has been working part-time in a café for more than three years. He brings that experience to what we do: he tests our products in everyday use and looks after the marketing. Thorsten is responsible for design, materials and product development, from the first sketch to the finished product.

Our mornings start at the espresso machine. Thorsten’s in Munich, Jonah’s in Vienna. The latte art doesn’t work out every day, but the coffee does.

We were inspired by the many lovely speciality coffee shops in Munich and Vienna. Often everything there is just right: the atmosphere, the materials and the small details, from the milk jug to the cup. That’s exactly the feeling we wanted to bring to our own coffee corner.

That’s how began.

We develop products for the coffee corner that work every day and look good doing it. In matt colours you don’t find everywhere. We pay attention to the small details, right down to the packaging: our products come without any plastic.

By the way, is inspired by the Scandinavian words ‘mjölk’ (milk) and ‘å’ (small river). If you’ve ever poured latte art, you might see why.
