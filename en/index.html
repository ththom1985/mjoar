350 millilitres. Deliberately no more.

Milk grows as you steam it. So the LUN holds more than ends up in the cup. Enough for a latte, a cappuccino, a matcha latte or two flat whites. For a jug you'll pick up every morning, that's the right size. Small enough to pour with control. Big enough for two.

How to steam milk with the LUN.

Cold milk, cold jug. Steam on, a moment of air, then the milk starts to turn. With a little practice, you'll feel on the body when it's ready. The handle stays comfortable. One tap, a swirl until it shines. Then pour.

Dishwasher? Yes.

All four colours can go in, on the days when there's no time. If you like yours, rinse it by hand and give it a quick dry. That keeps the finish free of water marks for longest.
